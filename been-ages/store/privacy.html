Been Ages（好久沒了）隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-13

Been Ages does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. We have no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking. In-app purchases are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Been Ages 不蒐集、傳送或分享任何個人資料。所有資料只存在你的裝置上。沒有帳號、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告，也沒有第三方追蹤。App 內購買由 Apple 處理，我們不會拿到你的付款資訊。刪除 App 就會清除所有資料。

通知權限 / Notification permission

If you allow notifications, they are used only for local reminders on this device (for example, when an item is due). We do not send push notifications from a server, and we do not use the permission for marketing.

若你允許通知，僅用於本機提醒（例如某件事到期時）。我們不會從伺服器發送推播，也不會拿這項權限做行銷。

JSON 匯出 / JSON export

Export writes a JSON file on your device. It is not uploaded to us. You choose whether and where to share that file (for example via the system share sheet).

匯出會在你的裝置上產生 JSON 檔，不會上傳到我們的伺服器。要不要分享、分享到哪裡，都由你決定（例如系統分享選單）。
