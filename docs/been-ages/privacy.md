# Been Ages — Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

Been Ages does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. We have no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking. In-app purchases are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Been Ages 不蒐集、傳送或分享任何個人資料。所有資料只存在你的裝置上。沒有帳號、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告，也沒有第三方追蹤。App 內購買由 Apple 處理，我們不會拿到你的付款資訊。刪除 App 就會清除所有資料。
