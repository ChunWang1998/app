# Been Ages — Support / 支援

Questions, bug reports, or purchase restore issues: email **tinytoolshedhq@gmail.com**.
To restore the Full Pack purchase: open Settings and tap **Restore purchases**, or tap **Restore** on the Full Pack purchase screen.

有問題、要回報錯誤，或購買無法恢復，請寄信到 **tinytoolshedhq@gmail.com**。
要恢復「完整版」購買：進入「設定」點「恢復購買」，或在完整版購買畫面點「恢復」。
